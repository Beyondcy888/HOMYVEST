# 02 — Project brief

## 1. HOMYVEST in one breath
HOMYVEST helps people whose land or building is sitting idle turn it into a property that earns. It shows them what their land is allowed to hold, what it could become, what it would cost and what it could earn, and what they must plan for. Then its partners build it (**HomyEight**) and run it (**Behomy**).

Think of HOMYVEST as a **permanent campaign**: an always-open invitation to owners of idle land and property.

## 2. What HOMYVEST is not
- **Not a developer, and it does not sell buildings or land.** Never write copy that implies HOMYVEST sells a building, a unit or a plot.
- Not limited to guesthouses or kost. The land decides: stays, offices, cafés, sports venues and more.
- Not a get-rich-quick pitch. Its credibility comes from honest numbers and real projects.

## 3. Origin (context only)
The idea grew from the **Build-to-Rent** model in Indonesia, where an operator and a builder team up so a landowner can build and rent out property. HOMYVEST has the same shape (HomyEight builds, Behomy operates) but differs in three ways the site should make *felt*, never stated as a list:
1. **Use-agnostic:** "what should *this* land become?" rather than "build a kost".
2. **Hybrid operations:** Behomy mixes daily and monthly stays, so rooms keep earning across seasons.
3. **More than one way in:** empty land, or an idle or unfinished building.

**Do not borrow anything** from the visual design of Rukita's Build-to-Rent page or bermbuild.com. Do not name or link them.

## 4. Who visits

### Persona A: the owner of empty land
Owns a plot with nothing on it. It costs them tax and upkeep and earns nothing. They don't know what they're allowed to build, what would earn the most, or what building and running a property involves. They're not architects or investors. **They need:** clarity on what's possible, believable numbers, reassurance that one team handles everything, and an easy first step.

### Persona B: the owner of an idle or unfinished building
Owns land with an old building, an unfinished building (*mangkrak*), or one that simply isn't earning. **They need:** to know whether the building can be completed or extended within the rules, what business would suit it, and that HomyEight can continue the construction.

### Secondary: capital-only investors
People without land who want to invest. Serve them with one FAQ answer pointing to Behomy's Equity Partnership. Don't build a section for them.

### What every visitor must leave knowing
Who we are → what we do → why us (without the word "USP") → what *their* land could become → exactly how to move forward.

## 8. Why us, shown and never labelled
The visitor should *conclude* these from the story, the visuals and the numbers. Never present them as a "Why choose us" list or use the word "USP":
1. **One team from empty land to monthly report.** HOMYVEST finds the potential, HomyEight builds, Behomy runs.
2. **The land decides, not our catalogue.** Stays, offices, cafés, sports halls.
3. **Operations that keep rooms full.** Hybrid stays, 82% average occupancy.
4. **Honest numbers first.** A real calculator with real rules, gross figures clearly labelled.
5. **Proof, not promises.** Real projects, built and run.

## 9. Tone of voice
Calm, warm, plain-spoken and confident. Short sentences. Speak to one owner ("your land"), never to "investors". No hype words ("revolutionary", "unlock", "skyrocket", "passive income machine"). No exclamation marks in body copy. Indonesian copy should sound natural and polite, using "Anda", never translated word for word. Technical terms (KDB, KLB, KDH, PBG) always come with a plain-language explanation the first time they appear.
